# Intraday data feasibility (phase P0 of `docs/intraday_research_plan.md`)

**Status:** research data only. No `src/`, config or live-bot change. Paper/research use.
Snapshot taken **2026-10-20 ~21:15 UTC**; every number below comes from `strategy_pack_run/data_intraday/coverage_summary.json`
(generated by `validate_intraday_p0.py`), not from memory.

## 1. Verdict in one paragraph

Deep 15m and 1h history **exists and is usable**, from one source: Coinbase Exchange public candles (USD quote). That is
3.73M 15m bars and 0.94M 1h bars over the 16 coins, back to the coin's first Coinbase trade (BTC 2015-07-20). Fifteen of
the sixteen coins have at least 3.2 years of it; **BNB has only one year** (listed on Coinbase 2025-10-22), so for BNB the P0
answer is "not available at the depth the plan asks for". Three things limit what a backtest can honestly claim:
(a) the history is **Coinbase prints, not Kraken prints** (Kraken is the venue the bot would trade; its public API can
only confirm the last 30 days of 1h and 7.5 days of 15m); (b) **the 15m feed of nine alt coins is internally inconsistent
in two windows** (2024-08-13..2024-09-03 and 2025-01-01..2025-04-30) and should be masked or replaced by the 1h feed there;
(c) **XRP has a 2.4-year hole** (Coinbase suspended it 2021-01-19 to 2023-07-13). Details and a recommendation are in
sections 5 and 6.

## 2. Sources and what was tried

| Source | Reachable here? | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Coinbase Exchange `api.exchange.coinbase.com/products/<BASE>-USD/candles` | yes, no key | **primary and only history source**. Serves 15m and 1h back to each product's first trade (verified for BTC back to 2016, 300 bars/request) |
| Kraken public OHLC (`api.kraken.com`, via ccxt) | yes | last ~720 bars per pair only, so used **only to cross-check recent bars** |
| Binance, Bybit, `data.binance.vision` | no (HTTP 000/451) | not usable from this environment |
| Trade-tape reconstruction for Kraken | not attempted | would mean paging Kraken's public trades endpoint from each coin's start and building candles locally; not attempted (large, slow, unscoped), left as an open option if Kraken-native history turns out to matter |

Coverage was never mixed across sources inside one coin, and nothing is interpolated or forward-filled.

## 3. What was built (all under `strategy_pack_run/`, nothing in `src/` or `config/`)

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `fetch_intraday_p0.py` | resumable parallel fetcher: explicit non-overlapping 300-bar windows, shared rate limiter (7 req/s used), exponential back-off on 429/5xx/network, appends per window, state file per (coin, timeframe), crash-safe de-duplication, closed bars only, UTC epoch-ms bar-open timestamp, schema `timestamp,open,high,low,close,volume` (same as `data_long/`). Each coin starts at its first **daily** bar in `data_long/` so late-listed coins are not probed from 2014. Re-running the same command tops the files up. |
| `validate_intraday_p0.py` | gap, sanity, 15m-vs-1h, 15m/1h-vs-daily, market-wide-outage and Kraken checks; writes `coverage_summary.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` |
| `test_fetch_intraday_p0.py`, `test_validate_intraday_p0.py` | 15 + 23 unit tests on hand-built data, no network |
| `data_intraday/coverage_summary.json` | committed, per coin and timeframe coverage/quality numbers |
| `data_intraday/SHA256SUMS.txt` | committed, SHA-256 of the 32 CSVs as fetched. The raw CSVs (about 240 MB) are gitignored. A regenerated snapshot will have extra trailing bars (top-up appends), so compare a prefix, not the whole file |

Reproduce: `python strategy_pack_run/fetch_intraday_p0.py --out strategy_pack_run/data_intraday --timeframes 1h 15m --workers 6 --rate 7`
(about 30 minutes for a cold fetch of all 32 files, about 16,000 requests; no job failed in the run documented here, retries are silent so the number of transient 429s was not recorded), then
`python strategy_pack_run/validate_intraday_p0.py --data-dir strategy_pack_run/data_intraday --kraken --summary-out strategy_pack_run/data_intraday/coverage_summary.json --sha-out strategy_pack_run/data_intraday/SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### Other known holes

* **XRP, 2021-01-19 18:00 to 2023-07-13 20:00 UTC, no bars** (both timeframes). This matches Coinbase suspending XRP trading and later

## 5. Quality findings

### 5.1 Basic sanity: clean

Across all 32 files: 0 duplicate timestamps, 0 unsorted rows, 0 zero-volume bars, 0 NaNs, 0 non-positive prices, and 0 OHLC
ordering violations (high below open/close/low, or low above open/close/high). Only liquidity-related flat bars (open=high=low=close)
exist: 1.8% of XTZ 15m bars, 1.6% of LTC 15m, 0.65% of ALGO and SHIB, under 0.5% elsewhere.

## 6. What the later backtests can honestly use, and recommendation

**Per-coin tiers.**

| Tier | Coins | Usable window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: use | BTC, ETH, SOL, LTC | 1h and 15m: from 2021-10-01 (or the full history for BTC/ETH/LTC, but pre-2019 books are thin and not comparable to today's) | cleanest feeds: 15m-vs-1h disagreement <=0.33% of hours; Kraken agreement median <=2.5bp. LTC has 3.2% missing 15m bars overall (mostly 2016-17); apply the 15m mask for LTC. BTC/ETH need no mask beyond the market-wide outages |
| B: use with the 15m mask | ADA, DOGE, AVAX, LINK, DOT, ATOM, BCH, SHIB, ALGO (from 2020-07), XRP (post-relisting) | 1h: full listing history. 15m: same, with the three windows below excluded or replaced by 1h-derived data | gaps are tiny outside the outages; Kraken median 1-8bp |
| C: 1h only, or sensitivity only | XTZ | 1h from 2019-09. Do **not** use 15m for a pass/fail gate | Kraken median 10.7-11.7bp (flagged), 1.3% missing and 1.8% flat 15m bars, worst 15m-vs-1h and 15m-vs-daily disagreement (29% of 2025 days) |
| D: special handling | XRP | continuous only from **2023-07-13 20:00**; earlier segment 2019-02-26..2021-01-19 is separate | the 905-day hole is Coinbase-only; do not stitch, do not forward-fill |
| E: exclude | BNB | n/a | only 363 days of history (2025-10-22 onward); fails the plan's 3-year bar and any walk-forward-by-year gate |

**15m masks (simplest uniform rule: apply to every coin except BTC and ETH; SOL needs only the 2023-03-04 outage day):** 2023-03-04, 2024-08-13..2024-09-03, 2025-01-01..2025-04-30. In those windows use the native 1h bars (they match the daily feed) or drop the days. Do not
resample 15m to build 1h/4h/1d for these coins unless the masks are applied, and **never mix native-1h and 15m-derived-1h in one run**.

**Honest common windows:**

* All 15 coins other than BNB: **2023-07-13 20:00 to now (about 3.27 years)**, bounded by XRP's relisting. This is the longest window with the plan's full universe, and it contains the two bad 15m periods above.
* 14 coins (drop XRP and BNB): **2021-10-01 to now (about 5.05 years)**, bounded by AVAX (2021-09-30) and SHIB (2021-09-09).
* BTC/ETH-only studies: from 2018-01 (ETH is thin before 2016-08; BTC is fine from 2015-08 but market structure then is not today's).

**Against the P0 pass condition ("at least 3 years of 15m/1h for the 16 coins, or a clear 'not available'"):** met for 15 coins at both timeframes (XRP only for its post-2023 segment as a continuous series); **not met for BNB**. The 3-year figure is a depth of
history, not a statement that the three years are clean: see the masks above.

**Do not claim from this data:**

* Anything about Kraken fills, spreads or capacity: this is Coinbase data, volumes are 1-3x Kraken's, and Kraken can only be spot-checked for 30 days.
* Anything that depends on bars during outages being tradable: during the windows in section 4 Coinbase had no price while Kraken may have. The harness (P1) must decide how a missing bar is handled and must not forward-fill it silently.
* Survivorship-free universe results. The 16 coins are today's survivors: Coinbase-listed, still on Kraken, chosen with hindsight. Delisted or failed assets are absent, and each coin's history starts at its Coinbase listing, so early-year results use a shrinking, selected universe (only BTC/ETH/LTC/BCH exist before 2019). The 3.27-year common window is the least affected by this; the 5-year window is not immune.

**Open items for the owner (not done in P0):** an independent historical venue to adjudicate the 2024-2025 15m inconsistency (none reachable from this environment); Kraken trade-tape reconstruction if Kraken-native history is required; whether the plan's 15m families should be run on the masked set or on 1h only.
